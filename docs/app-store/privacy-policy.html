AdPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-21

AdPlay (“the App”) is operated by the developer publishing the App (“we”). This policy describes what data we collect and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers — anonymous device id and/or Sign in with Apple subject, used to store game progress and balance.
  • Gameplay data — progress, taps, boost timers, sat ledger entries, withdrawal requests (including Lightning invoices you submit).
  • Diagnostics — basic server logs (IP, timestamps, error codes) to operate and secure the service.
  • Advertising (when enabled) — third-party ad partners may collect device advertising identifiers and usage data subject to their policies and your ATT/consent choices.

How we use data

  • Operate the progress-bar game and sat ledger.
  • Process redemption requests you submit.
  • Prevent fraud and enforce daily caps.
  • Comply with law and App Store / Play policies.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure providers (hosting) and, when live ads are enabled, ad/offer partners required to serve and verify rewarded completions. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Account and ledger data are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion (see below).

Your choices

  • Request account deletion via in-app support or by contacting the email listed on the store listing.
  • Decline optional rewarded ads; core tap gameplay remains available within daily tap limits.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 17.
