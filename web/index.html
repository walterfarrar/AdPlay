How to redeem

What you need

AdPlay pays Bitcoin over the Lightning Network. You’ll need a Lightning wallet that can create a receive invoice (a BOLT11 string starting with lnbc).

Steps

  1. Earn sats by filling the progress bar completely on the home screen.
  2. Open a Lightning wallet and create a receive invoice for the exact amount you want.
  3. Copy the BOLT11 invoice from your wallet.
  4. In AdPlay Redeem, enter that amount, paste the invoice, and submit.
  5. We pay invoices manually. Watch History for pending → paid (or rejected / refunded).

Apps you can use

Any Lightning wallet that creates BOLT11 invoices works. Popular options:

  • Wallet of Satoshi — simple, good for beginners
  • Phoenix — self-custodial Lightning wallet
  • Zeus — full-featured Lightning wallet
  • Alby — browser and mobile Lightning wallet
  • Muun — Bitcoin + Lightning
  • Strike — Bitcoin payments with Lightning
  • Cash App — Lightning receive where available

Tips

Match the invoice amount to what you enter here. Invoices expire, so create a fresh one when you’re ready to submit. Only one pending withdrawal is allowed at a time.
